# Code of conduct

This applies to everyone taking part in pandera-chem: issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews.

## What we expect

- Be welcoming and patient. People come from many countries, backgrounds and levels of experience;
  every question asked in good faith is a good question.
- Be respectful when you disagree. Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- Credit other people's work, and do not present it as your own.
- Keep it on topic: no spam, no advertising.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind, including on the basis of nationality,
  ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or level of experience.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, or encouraging harm.
- Publishing other people's private information.

## Reporting

Write to [contact@monstrous.com.ng](mailto:contact@monstrous.com.ng). Reports are read by the
maintainer and kept confidential. People who break this code may be warned, have comments removed,
or be blocked from the project.
